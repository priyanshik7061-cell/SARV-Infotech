Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist, author and the 11th President of India.

About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He made significant contributions to India's space and defence programmes and became one of India's most respected scientific and public figures.

His Admirable Qualities

Visionary Thinking

Dr. Kalam encouraged young people to dream big and work towards a better future.

Dedication

His dedication to science, education and the nation made him an inspiration to millions.

Humility

Despite his achievements, he was known for his simple and humble personality.

Inspiration

He inspired students to learn, dream and contribute positively to society.

Major Achievements

Space Programme

He played an important role in India's space programme and worked with ISRO.

Missile Development

He made major contributions to India's missile development programmes.

President of India

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.
